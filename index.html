      How it works

      A sensor panel in the bunk. Nothing is worn.

      Sleep with bare calves on the strip. The rest happens without you.

      Impedance strip under the calves

      Dry electrodes in the mattress pass a tiny current through the calf and read the muscle.

      Radar above the bunk

      Reads breathing rate and heart rate from chest movement. No contact, no camera.

      Particle trackers above and below

      Record each cosmic-ray particle that crosses the bunk: the night's dose, and where it came from.
