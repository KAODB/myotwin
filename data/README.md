# Real data replayed by MyoTwin

These four small files hold real NASA measurements. `js/sim.js` replays them
night by night as the radiation dose of Moon and Mars nights. Each file is a
plain script that registers `MyoTwin.data.<name>` in the browser and exports the
same object in Node, with `name`, `source`, `url`, `retrieved`, `units`, `processing`,
`terms` and the rows. Each is generated by a standard-library Python script in
`tools/data/` from the raw downloads, which are not in this repository (they are
large). Run the scripts with `python3 -I` from the repository root, for example
`python3 -I tools/data/build_lro_crater.py <raw>/doserates_nospe.txt <raw>/doserates_standard_2017262_31days_allevents.txt data/lro-crater.js`.
Nothing is interpolated or smoothed. All files were retrieved on 2026-11-14.
`test/data.test.js` checks row counts, date order, ranges and gaps, and
recomputes every number of evidence entries V60-V63 from these files.

## `msl-rad.js` (`MyoTwin.data.mslRad`): Curiosity RAD, Mars surface

- **Source:** NASA Planetary Data System, PPI node, dataset MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0
  (MSL Radiation Assessment Detector, reduced data records).
- **URLs:** https://pds-ppi.igpp.ucla.edu/data/MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0/ ; data files
  under `DATA/SOL_00000_00089/` and `DATA/SOL_00090_00179/`, one
  `RAD_RDR_<YYYY>_<DDD>_<HH>_<MM>_<SSSS>_V00.TXT` per sol.
- **Rows:** 94, one per sol from sol 13 to sol 112: sol, UTC date of the first
  observation, median of `DOSIMETRY_TOTAL_DOSE_B` over all observations of that
  sol (uGy/h, rounded to 0.001), number of observations.
- **Units:** absorbed dose rate in silicon detector B (label UNIT "microGray / Hour").
- **Processing:** the dosimetry fields were extracted from the sol files
  (about 44 observations per sol, 1920 s each); per sol, the median of detector
  B. The median is used because a few readings are near zero and a recurring
  midday pattern (B about 14, E about 24 uGy/h) appears in about 40
  observations; the cause is not confirmed. Script: `tools/data/build_msl_rad.py`.
- **Caveats:** sols 29-33 and 64 have no file in the PDS tree and are skipped,
  not filled (`missingSols`). The documents read describe no tissue-equivalence
  correction: this is dose in silicon, not in water or tissue. 8 observations
  carry a placeholder UTC stamp (`2013-123 21:52:00`); their dose values are
  kept and their stamps are ignored for the date. The day-night difference is
  about 1%, so the per-sol median stands in for a night.
- **Terms:** PDS data are public (NASA science information policy,
  https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/science-data/science-information-policy/).
  Cite: Hassler D.M. et al., MSL RAD Reduced Data Records,
  MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0, NASA Planetary Data System. Instrument paper: Hassler et
  al. 2012, Space Sci Rev 170:503.

## `lro-crater.js` (`MyoTwin.data.lroCrater`): LRO CRaTER, Moon

- **Source:** LRO CRaTER (Cosmic Ray Telescope for the Effects of Radiation)
  dose-rate products, instrument team, University of New Hampshire, NASA Lunar
  Reconnaissance Orbiter mission.
- **URLs:**
  https://crater-web.sr.unh.edu/data/craterProducts/doserates/doserates_nospe.txt
  (hourly, 2009 to 2026; despite its name it includes solar particle events) and
  https://crater-web.sr.unh.edu/data/craterProducts/doserates/data/2017262/doserates_standard_2017262_31days_allevents.txt
  (hourly, 2017-08-20 to 2017-09-19). Data page:
  https://crater-web.sr.unh.edu/products.php
- **Rows:** one per night for two windows: `quiet`, nights starting 2017-04-21
  to 2017-06-19 (60), and `event`, nights starting 2017-08-21 to 2017-10-05
  (46; contains the September 2017 events). Per night: start date (UTC), dose
  over 22:00-06:00 UTC in uGy behind detector pair D5&6 and behind D1&2
  (rounded to 0.1 uGy).
- **Units:** the file header says: "All dose rate units: cGy / day. All dose
  rates have a silicon to water correction applied and are altitude corrected
  to the lunar surface." Each hourly value contributes cGy/day x 1e4 / 24 uGy.
- **Processing:** the eight hourly values whose hour starts at 22:00 ... 05:00
  UTC are summed; a night with fewer than eight is dropped and listed in
  `droppedNights` (none were). The first file lacks 41 hours (2017-09-02 23:00
  to 2017-09-05 23:00); they come from the 31-day file. Where both files have an
  hour the values are identical; the script stops if they ever differ. Script:
  `tools/data/build_lro_crater.py`.
- **Caveats:** measured in lunar orbit and scaled to the surface by the
  provider (the altitude factor, about 0.70-0.79, accounts for the Moon blocking
  about half the sky). Dose in water after the provider's silicon-to-water
  factor of 1.333. D1&2 sits at the zenith end behind the thinnest shielding;
  D5&6 sits behind both tissue-equivalent plastic blocks (about 81 mm, a figure
  from Spence et al. 2010, not re-read from a file). D5&6 is used as a stand-in
  for someone inside a habitat; it is not a habitat measurement. The 22:00-06:00
  UTC sleep window is our assumption. The quiet window was chosen by
  inspection: no daily D1&2 mean in day-of-year 60-260 of 2017 exceeds 0.031
  cGy/day apart from 07-14 to 07-30 and 09-04 to 09-17.
- **Terms:** no licence text appears on crater-web.sr.unh.edu; NASA science
  data are open (NASA science information policy above). Cite the instrument
  team: Spence et al. 2010, Space Sci Rev 150:243; Schwadron et al. 2012, JGR
  117:E00H13. The archival CRaTER data are at NASA PDS PPI (LRO-L-CRAT-*).

## `donki-sep-2017.js` (`MyoTwin.data.donkiSep2017`): DONKI SEP records

- **Source:** NASA Community Coordinated Modeling Center (CCMC), DONKI, solar
  energetic particle (SEP) records, through the NASA Open APIs gateway.
- **URL:** https://api.nasa.gov/DONKI/SEP?startDate=2017-09-01&endDate=2017-09-30
  (requested with the public DEMO_KEY, which is not stored here).
- **Rows:** the 10 SEP records returned: id, start time (UTC), instrument
  names, linked event ids, unchanged, sorted by start time. `coverage` is the
  span the query asked for.
- **Caveats:** the DONKI flare (FLR) endpoint returned HTTP 404 twice and the
  CCMC endpoint timed out, so flare ids are kept only as ids linked from SEP
  records. Outside `coverage` the absence of a record means "not checked". The
  same physical event can appear as several records from different instruments
  (for example `2017-09-10T16:25:00-SEP-001` above 100 MeV and
  `2017-09-10T16:45:00-SEP-001` above 10 MeV). The simulator counts only
  records from GOES instruments (geostationary orbit, near Earth) for its
  solar-particle-event flag; records from STEREO A (far from the Earth-Moon
  system), SOHO (at the Sun-Earth L1 point) and models are kept in the file but
  not counted. Script: `tools/data/build_donki_sep.py`.
- **Terms:** DONKI is public; acknowledge "CCMC DONKI" (https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/).

## `omni-protons.js` (`MyoTwin.data.omniProtons`): OMNI2 proton flux

- **Source:** NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, Space Physics Data Facility
  (SPDF), OMNI2 low-resolution (hourly) dataset.
- **URLs:** https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/omni/low_res_omni/omni2_2017.dat
  (data) and https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/omni/low_res_omni/omni2.text (format).
- **Rows:** 312 hours, 2017-09-04T00Z to 2017-09-16T23Z: hour (UTC start) and
  integral proton flux above 10 MeV in protons per (cm2 s sr) (pfu).
- **Processing:** word 46 copied as given; fill values (99999.99) would become
  null (none occur in this span). Script: `tools/data/build_omni_protons.py`.
- **Caveats:** measured by GOES at geostationary orbit (per the OMNI
  documentation), not at the Moon. Not used by the simulator; it is cited as
  independent confirmation of the event (evidence V63).
- **Terms:** NASA SPDF data are public; acknowledge "OMNI data from GSFC/SPDF
  OMNIWeb" (https://omniweb.gsfc.nasa.gov/html/citing.html).
